# Meteorology/climate-science review brief

This brief is for an independent meteorology or climate-science reviewer. It is a request for scientific review, not evidence that review has occurred.

## Study target requiring review

The manuscript classifies daily Tmax at selected 1-degree grid-cell centers. For each cell and calendar day, its normal is the mean 1981--2010 Tmax smoothed with a centered 15-day circular window; a normal is used only when at least 10 distinct reference years contribute. Departure is daily Tmax minus that normal. Entry thresholds are 40 C for plains, 30 C for hills, and 37 C for coastal cells. After the entry threshold, plains/hill events use departure >=4.5 C or absolute Tmax >=45 C; severe events use departure >6.4 C or absolute Tmax >=47 C. Coastal events use departure >=4.5 C and severe events departure >6.4 C. Ordinary and severe events are both positive in the binary prediction target. The cited IMD FAQ describes official declarations using station counts within a meteorological subdivision over consecutive days; this study instead labels each grid-cell day independently and does not implement those declaration rules.

## Questions for the reviewer

1. Are the absolute and departure thresholds, terrain-specific application, and treatment of severe events correctly represented for the cited IMD-aligned grid-cell adaptation?
2. Is the 1981--2010 normal and centered 15-day circular smoothing scientifically defensible for this target? Are the >=10 reference-year support rule and missing-data exceptions acceptable?
3. Does assigning a cell label at the grid center create a meaningful interpretation problem or bias for coastal, hilly, or boundary cells?
4. Is the statement about the IMD gridded product's station-coverage change around 2008 appropriately limited, without attributing the observed prevalence change to coverage alone?
5. Is the CPC external-product sensitivity defensible when 0.5-degree daily Tmax analyses are averaged to 1-degree cells, the primary IMD 1981--2010 normal is retained, and underlying GTS reports may overlap with IMD observations?
6. Which conclusions should be narrowed, and are any essential sensitivity analyses missing?
